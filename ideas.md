## Selected Approach: Swiss Style (International Typographic Style)

I will proceed with the **Swiss Style** approach. It perfectly balances the "minimalist" request with the "academic/researcher" persona. The focus on clarity, grid systems, and strong typography suits a computer science professor and researcher. The "Electric Blue" accent will subtly nod to her work in "City Brain" and "Data".

**Why this choice?**
- It's professional and clean, fitting for an academic portfolio.
- It's timeless and won't look dated quickly.
- It allows the content (research, bio) to be the hero.
- It meets the "minimalist" requirement without being boring.
